About & How to Use Naturally Tagalog

Complete Guide to Language Islands, Repetition & Daily Practice

The Secret: Repetition Until Fully Learned

Naturally Tagalog is built around vivid sound-alike mnemonics and deliberate repetition—never dry grammar memorization. Every new word or conversational sentence becomes permanent only when you read it, listen to it, and speak it aloud repeatedly until it moves from unfamiliar to automatic.

Growing Curriculum & Regular Updates

Your deck continues to expand steadily with at least 100 new conversational sentences and 50 additional core words per update, organized across real-world Philippine situations—from wet markets and restaurants to condo errands, travel, and immigration.

1. Navigating with the Language Island Bar (Top Bar)

The horizontal Language Island Bar just below the header lets you focus on one everyday situation at a time (such as 1. Greetings & Small Talk, 2. Food, Ordering & Restaurants, or 4. Shopping & Money). Tapping any topic chip immediately filters your flashcards, daily training modules, and audio playlist to that island, while displaying your live mastery progress bar right inside each chip.

2. How to Rate Each Word & Sentence (Bottom of Every Card)

Every card starts in the red Again queue. As you practice each word or sentence over and over, tap one of the three buttons at the bottom of its card to schedule spaced repetition and update your top progress bar:

Again (5m)

Still new or forgotten. Keep repeating this word/sentence in short 5-minute loops until the mnemonic hook clicks.

Familiar (1d)

You recognize it, but need more reps. Review it again tomorrow to strengthen recall speed.

Learned (30d)

Fully mastered! You can understand and speak it naturally without hesitating.

3. Daily Training System (Four 15-Minute Repetition Modes)

Select any Language Island at the top, then work through the four daily training modules to lock words into long-term memory:

  • New Words (15 min): Introduces 5 words at a time with pronunciation audio, vivid mnemonic stories, and real-life Manila context.
  • Recall (15 min): Active-recall flashcards that hide the English meaning first so your brain works to retrieve it before grading Again, Familiar, or Learned.
  • Shadowing (15 min): Plays phrases at 0.75x slow speed and 1.0x natural speed—repeat out loud alongside the audio to train your mouth and rhythm.
  • Listening (15 min): Hands-free continuous audio loop that plays each Tagalog phrase aloud and advances automatically so you can absorb phrases while walking or commuting.

4. Complete Guide to Every Button & Tab

Focus Cues Button (Top Header): Toggles color-coded highlights for ang (topic/actor), ng (object/possessive), and sa (direction/location) markers so you naturally absorb Tagalog sentence structure.
Natural / Literal Toggle: Switches sentence cards between idiomatic natural English and word-for-word Tagalog structure so you see exactly how ideas are built.
Audio Speed Toggle (1.0x / 0.75x): Slows down every speaker button across the entire app so you can hear glottal stops and syllable stress clearly.
Needs Practice Flag: Flags difficult trouble spots into a dedicated Needs Practice deck and prioritizes them in your daily training.
Star Icon (On Cards): Bookmarks favorite phrases into your personal Favorites tab.
+ Add Content & My Islands: Import new lesson JSON packs, build your own custom Language Islands, or paste custom Tagalog | English pairs.
Tip: Read and speak each card aloud 3–5 times before moving it to Learned.
