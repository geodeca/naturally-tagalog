About & How to Use Naturally Tagalog

Complete Guide to Spaced Repetition, Buttons & Daily Practice

The Secret: Repetition Until Fully Learned

Naturally Tagalog is built around vivid sound-alike mnemonics and deliberate repetition—never dry grammar drills. Every new word or conversational sentence sticks permanently only when you hear it, say it aloud, and test yourself repeatedly until it moves from unfamiliar to automatic.

1. How to Rate Each Word & Sentence (Bottom of Every Card)

Every card starts in the red Again queue. As you practice each word or sentence over and over, tap one of the three buttons at the bottom of its card to track your mastery and update your top progress bar:

Again (5m)

Still new or forgotten. Keep repeating this word/sentence in short 5-minute loops until the mnemonic hook clicks.

Familiar (1d)

You recognize it, but need more reps. Review it again tomorrow to strengthen recall speed.

Learned (30d)

Fully mastered! You can understand and speak it naturally without hesitating.

2. Daily Training System (Four 15-Minute Repetition Modes)

Select any topic island at the top, then work through the four daily training modules to lock words into long-term memory:

  • New Words (15 min): Introduces 5 words at a time with pronunciation audio, vivid mnemonic stories, and real-life Manila context.
  • Recall (15 min): Active-recall flashcards that hide the English meaning first so your brain works to retrieve it before grading Again, Familiar, or Learned.
  • Shadowing (15 min): Plays phrases at 0.75x slow speed and 1.0x natural speed—repeat out loud alongside the audio to train your mouth and rhythm.
  • Listening (15 min): Hands-free continuous audio loop that plays each Tagalog phrase aloud and advances automatically so you can absorb phrases while walking or commuting.

3. Complete Guide to Every Button & Tab

Audio Speed Toggle (1.0x / 0.75x): Slows down every speaker button across the entire app so you can hear glottal stops and syllable contractions clearly.
Focus Cues (Ang / Ng / Sa): Color-codes Tagalog focus markers in sentences so you intuitively see how the sentence pivots.
Needs Practice Flag: Flags difficult trouble spots into a dedicated Needs Practice deck for focused review.
Literal / Natural Toggle: Switches sentence cards between natural English and word-for-word Tagalog structure.
Star Icon (On Cards): Bookmarks favorite phrases into your personal Favorites tab.
+ Add Content: Import new weekly lesson JSON packs or paste your own custom Tagalog | English word pairs.
Tip: Repeat each card aloud 3–5 times before moving it to Learned.
